About us

Built strong, built to last

SLJ Enterprises is a manufacturer of street lighting poles and related structures. We fabricate every pole with careful material selection, accurate welding and a protective surface finish so it stands up to wind, rain and years of outdoor use.

From a single project lot to large municipal and contractor orders, we work closely with each customer on specifications, drawings and delivery schedules.
