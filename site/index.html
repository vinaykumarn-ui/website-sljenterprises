About us

From concrete blocks to electric poles

SLJ Enterprises was started in 2015 with the help of family and friends. We began by manufacturing concrete blocks of 4, 6 and 8 inches and have served many contractors, builders and the public.

In December 2024 we started pole manufacturing at Dabaspet. We hold vendor licences from the BESCOM and CESC Mysore divisions, have supplied more than 10,000 poles to date, and have a production capacity of 60 poles per day.

Approval

Approved vendor to BESCOM
